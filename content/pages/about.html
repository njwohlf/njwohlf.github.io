About me

I’m Nick, a software engineer in Ann Arbor. I like writing code, working with robots, and having a new problem to figure out.

I graduated from the University of Michigan in 2024 with a degree in Computer Science Engineering. While I was there, I joined research labs studying robotics, simulation, and control systems.

I also spent a lot of time tutoring programming and helping with courses. Explaining something to someone else is a good way to find out how well you understand it yourself.

These days, I work in U-M’s Robotics Department, where I help improve web interfaces alongside the department’s administrative work. Outside that role, I keep exploring software and personal projects.
