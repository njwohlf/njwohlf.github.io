Orbital Satellite Simulation

  • Simulation
  • Dynamics
  • Robotics

In the VODCA Lab, I helped develop a satellite orbit simulation using an omnidirectional ground rover as a physical testbed. My work connected orbital models to robot commands and supported real-time data collection and analysis.

The rover provided a way to study aspects of orbital motion in a lab, within the limits of what a ground robot can represent.
