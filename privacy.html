Privacy

This portfolio is designed to be useful without analytics, advertising scripts, or an account.

Messages you send

The contact form sends your name, email address, and message to Formspree for delivery. I use that information to read and respond to your inquiry. Please avoid sending passwords, financial details, or other sensitive information.

Formspree processes form submissions under its privacy policy.

Browser preferences

If you change the appearance or project layout, this site saves that preference in your browser’s local storage. Those preferences are not sent to me. You can remove them by clearing this site’s browser data. The site remains usable when local storage is unavailable.

Hosting and external links

Your browser sends standard request information to the website’s host when loading pages and files. Following an external link takes you to a separate service with its own privacy practices.

Questions or requests

For questions about information you’ve sent, or to request its deletion from my correspondence, use the contact form.
